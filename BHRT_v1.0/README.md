\# Beagle High Resolution Template (BHRT)



\*\*Version:\*\* 1.0  

\*\*Update:\*\* 2026-09-22



\---



\## Introduction



BHRT is a high-quality beagle brain template.



\---



\## Files



\### T1w Template



\- `BHRT\_T1w\_0.5mm.nii.gz` — T1w template

\- `BHRT\_T1w\_brain\_0.5mm.nii.gz` — T1w template with brain tissues only



\### T2w Template



\- `BHRT\_T2w\_0.5mm.nii.gz` — T2w template

\- `BHRT\_T2w\_brain\_0.5mm.nii.gz` — T2w template with brain tissues only



\### Myelin Map



\- `BHRT\_MyelinMap\_0.5mm.nii.gz` — Myelin map

\- `BHRT\_MyelinMap\_brain\_0.5mm.nii.gz` — Myelin map with brain tissues only



\### Brain Mask and Tissue Segmentation



\- `BHRT\_BrainMask\_0.5mm.nii.gz` — Whole brain mask

\- `BHRT\_TissueSegment\_0.5mm.nii.gz` — Three types of brain tissues (CSF = 1, GM = 2, WM = 3)

\- `BHRT\_TPM\_0.5mm.nii.gz` — Tissue probability maps with three types

\- `BHRT\_TPM\_CSF\_0.5mm.nii.gz` — Tissue probability map of CSF

\- `BHRT\_TPM\_GM\_0.5mm.nii.gz` — Tissue probability map of GM

\- `BHRT\_TPM\_WM\_0.5mm.nii.gz` — Tissue probability map of WM



\### Cortical and Subcortical Atlas



\- `BHRT\_atlas\_cort+subcort\_0.5mm.nii.gz` — Cortical and subcortical atlas

\- `Label\_BHRT\_atlas\_cort+subcort.txt` — Label information of cortical and subcortical atlas



\### White Matter Atlas



\- `BHRT\_atlas\_wm\_0.5mm.nii.gz` — White matter atlas

\- `Label\_BHRT\_atlas\_wm.txt` — Label information of white matter atlas



\### Lobe Atlas



\- `BHRT\_atlas\_lobe\_0.5mm.nii.gz` — Lobe atlas

\- `Label\_BHRT\_atlas\_lobe.txt` — Label information of lobe atlas
